# The day's verse and hadith

- verses.json: the Quran's text (Hafs, simple script with diacritics) from https://github.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api
  (edition ara-quransimple, The Unlicense), picked by reference; nothing is typed by hand.
- hadith.json: from al-Nawawi's Forty Hadith (al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyya), Arabic text from
  https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api (edition ara-nawawi, The Unlicense). Each entry is the Prophet's words
  cut from that text between markers, with the companion who narrated it and where it is recorded, as al-Nawawi gives.
